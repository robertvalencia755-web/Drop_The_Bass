### Drop The Bass
Created By - Robert Valencia, Rhett Wood, Ryder Anderson, Kolby Carpenter

# Description
Drop The Bass is an open world fishing rhythm game! Catch fish around the world to add to your inventory, upgrade your gear, and catch better fish.

The twist is in order to catch fish, you must play a rhythm game! Hit notes using your mouse to control a net, and reach the goal to successfully capture the fish on your reel. 


## How To Play

Movement - WASD
Talk - E
Continue / Skip Speech - Space
Fish - F
Pause - Escape

# ---CHEAT CODES---
ALT + U - Equip Testing Rod
ALT + I - Equip Medium Difficulty Rod
ALT + O - Equip Hard Difficulty Rod
ALT + G - Equip God Bait (100% catch chance)
ALT + F - Equip Default Bait
